02 · Dug from the archives

Vintage packs

Crackly 78s from the 1920s, the birth of jazz, swing and dance-band drumming, brought back to life as fresh loops and one-shots. Every source record is old enough to be in the public domain, so there is nothing to clear and no royalties to pay: use these sounds anywhere, commercial releases included.

  1. Dig. I pull original transfers of 1920s records from public archives.
  2. Check. Each record's rights are checked one by one before it goes in a pack.
  3. Split. AI pulls the drums, bass, horns and vocal hooks apart into clean stems.
  4. Cut. I chop the best breaks, hits and hooks by ear and tag them with tempo.

How downloads work

Tracks and albums are on Bandcamp. Sample packs land here as they are finished. Until instant checkout is switched on, sample packs are paid by PayPal or Interac e-Transfer and the download link comes by email.

What you can do with them

Use the sounds in your own released music, no credit needed. Just do not resell or share the raw files.
