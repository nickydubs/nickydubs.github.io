03 · Live

Catch me live

Edmonton drummer turned basshead. Acoustic drums meet festival bass music, built live with loops.

Nicky Dubs plays jungle, drum & bass, dubstep and house on a real drum kit, layering it live with a loop station until it hits like a festival bass set. Headliner at InterSECT Festival three years running (2021–2023), twelve sets at Astral Harvest across 2018–2019 including a solo set in 2019, support slots for Dub FX, Benny Page and Ed Solo, and thirteen three-hour shows with Ann Vriend fronting Tiger Coffee, and on stage with the Jungle Cakes crew.

04 · Play

Wiggle some air yourself

A beat pad and synth you can play right here in your browser, watched over by the Air Wiggler, a little robot that dances to whatever you play. 16 pads, seven kits (Soul MPC, 808 Trap, Jungle, Liquid DnB, Dubstep, House, Roller Disco), eight ready-made beats to jam over, and a metronome. Tap the pads or use your keyboard (1–4 · Q–R · A–F · Z–V), and record your own loops into the 8 slots underneath (5–8 · T–I). Right-click a pad to load your own sample.
